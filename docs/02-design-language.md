# 02 — Design Language: "The Chart Room"

> A navigator's chart table on the night before a voyage. Paper charts held flat with brass weights, ink lines, dividers, a compass rose, carved wooden pieces for each crew member, red sealing wax. Everything is **made of something**. Nothing glows.

This fits HackGT 13's seaside theme (tracks are named Oracle of the Deep, The Shipyard, The Lighthouse Laboratory, A Marina's Mission) without being a costume: the metaphor *is* the product — a crew planning a voyage around a chart, each with a say, sealing the log when agreed.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Material, not digital.** Every surface is paper, ink, brass, wood, glass, wax or cloth. If you can't name the material, redesign it.
2. **Crew, not chatbot.** Agents are crew members with a carved piece, a name flag and a voice. They never "type", never "think", never appear in bubbles.
3. **Physical motion.** Things slide, tip, unroll, press, tick. Nothing floats, pulses, shimmers or fades in from nowhere.
4. **Private things look sealed.** Anything private (a Brief, a cap) is shown as a sealed letter or a closed brass case. Anything shared is ink on the common chart.
5. **Numbers are logged, not decorated.** Prices appear in a mono "ship's log" face, right‑aligned, like a ledger.
6. **Legible first.** Headset text must be readable at arm's length through phone‑VR lenses (the iPhone in the Gear VR shell, ~13 px per degree) and, if we get one, in mixed reality on a Quest 3. Style never beats readability.

---

## 2. The anti‑AI rulebook (hard bans)

| Banned | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Purple/blue/pink gradients, neon, "aurora" backgrounds | Flat chart paper, ink, brass; subtle paper grain |
| Glowing orbs, pulsing blobs, particle swirls as "the AI" | Carved wooden crew pieces |
| Sparkle ✨ icons, "magic" wand, stars on buttons | Plain verbs: "Seal", "Set sail", "Weigh anchor" |
| Chat bubbles, typing dots "…" | **Ink ribbons** (handwritten lines drawn under a speaking piece) + captions in the log |
| Glassmorphism, frosted panels, blur‑behind cards | Paper cards with a torn/deckled edge, brass frames |
| Robot/brain/circuit icons | Crew flag with the member's initial |
| "AI is thinking…" spinners | A pencil scratching / dividers walking across the chart |
| "Generated by AI", "Powered by AI" badges in UI | Credits live on the Devpost/About page only |
| Emoji as iconography | Engraved line icons (see §6) |
| Perfectly smooth 3D blobs, glossy plastic | Faceted wood, matte paper, brushed brass |
| Words: "assistant", "copilot", "genie", "AI‑powered", "smart" | "Mate", "crew", "Captain", "chart", "log" |

**Review gate:** before every merge that touches UI, check the screen against this table. Any hit = fix before demo.

## 4. Typography

| Role | Typeface (Google Fonts, free) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Display / headings | **Libre Caslon Display** (large) + **Libre Caslon Text** (≤ 28px) | 18th‑c. printing, chart cartouches |
| Body / UI | **Source Serif 4** | Sturdy serif that stays readable at small sizes; avoids the default "SaaS sans" look |
| Numbers, prices, times, codes | **IBM Plex Mono** | Ship's‑log ledger; tabular figures |
| Handwriting (ink ribbons, margin notes) | **Homemade Apple** *(only for ≤ 8 words; captions repeat it in Source Serif)* | Human hand, clearly not a chatbot font |

| Token | Size / LH | Face |
|---|---|---|
| `display` | 34 / 38 | Caslon Display |
| `h1` | 26 / 32 | Caslon Text |
| `h2` | 20 / 26 | Caslon Text |
| `body` | 17 / 26 | Source Serif 4 |

| `num` | 16 / 22 | Plex Mono, tabular |

### 4.2 XR text rules (VR chart room; Quest 3 MR)
- Render text with **troika‑three‑text** (SDF, crisp at any distance).
- **Minimum cap height 12 mm at ~0.6 m** viewing distance (≈ 1.1° visual angle); headings ≥ 20 mm.
- **VR chart room (primary: iPhone in a Gear VR shell).** The phone gives each eye roughly 13 px per degree after the lenses, so 1.1° is about 14 px: the floor, not a comfortable size. The whole tabletop is scaled **×1.4** in VR (chart centre 0.9 m ahead, 0.5 m below the eye), so the same labels read larger than on a Quest. Floating cards (menu, hail card) sit ahead of the eye, nearly level, clear of the gaze down at the table. As built some labels are still under the 12 mm target (README *Known limits*); check them through the shell, not on a laptop.
- Max 8 words per ink ribbon; full sentence goes to the caption strip on the chart edge.
- Text always on a paper card with 6 mm margin; never on the bare room walls (VR) or raw passthrough (Quest).
- Text faces the user (billboard on Y axis only; no roll).

## 5. Materials & textures

| Material | Implementation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Chart paper | Tileable 512px paper texture (grain + faint fibers), `MeshStandardMaterial` roughness 0.95 | Also CSS background on phones (≤ 60 KB WebP) |
| Ink lines | `Line2` (fat lines) in `--ink`, 1.5–2.5 mm world width; slight dash irregularity | Graticules, routes, borders |
| Brass | Roughness 0.35, metalness 1.0, color `--brass`; no environment‑mapped mirror shine | Frames, clock, compass, dial |
| Wood | Faceted low‑poly walnut, roughness 0.6 | Crew pieces, table rim |
| Glass | Cloche: transmission 0.9, roughness 0.05, thin rim highlight only | Dry Run domes; **no blur, no frosted glass** |
| Sealing wax | Red, roughness 0.4, slight bump map with crest | Seals |
| Cloth | Dark green baize under the chart, roughness 1.0 | Grounds the chart on the walnut table (VR) or the real table (Quest passthrough) |

Lighting: one warm key light (2700K feel) from above‑left like an oil lamp (`DirectionalLight` #FFDCAE) + a warm hemisphere fill (#FFF1DC over #3B3026) and a faint baked room environment for the brass and glass; on a Quest, ambient from the passthrough estimate if available. The same lights serve the Gallery and the VR room. No rim lights, no bloom.

### 5.1 The VR chart room (the headset)
The headset is VR only (an iPhone in a Gear VR shell, doc 10), so there's no real table to sit on. The chart gets a room of its own:
- **Dark room.** A closed, windowless room in `room` #221C17 (the Gallery's background), walls a shade darker and unlit, so there's nothing to read, no banding and no cost. The room exists to frame the table, not to be looked at: no windows, shelves or props competing with the chart.
- **Walnut table.** A round walnut tabletop (`woodDark` #4A3120, faceted, roughness 0.65), Ø ≈ 1.3 m, on a single pedestal, with the baize and chart on top; walnut floor below. Seated height: chart 0.5 m below the eye, 0.9 m ahead.
- **Lamplight.** The warm key + hemisphere above: the table and pieces are lit, the room falls off to dark around them, like a chart table under an oil lamp at night.
- **Fixtures of the room.** A red paper **Exit VR** plaque straight below the eyes (look down to leave); a small **Hail the table** paper tag on the table's near‑left edge (only while hails are open); the brass ship's wheel (menu) on the chart's south‑east edge. All are paper, brass or wax, per §1.
- **Reticle.** A small paper‑white dot at the centre of view that turns **Sounding Red** over a target; a thin Sounding Red ring fills round it for the 1.6 s dwell, and an outer **brass** ring fills for the 3.2 s recenter on the wheel. No glow, no pulsing.
- **Budget.** The room is 4 draw calls. Everything stays within an easy downward glance and ~40° either side (seated, 3DoF).

*On a Quest (optional):* the room is not drawn. Passthrough shows the real room, and the chart lies on the real table (§7).

## 7. XR component library (the scene)

All dimensions in real‑world units. In the VR chart room (the primary headset) the scene sits on the walnut table at a fixed seated pose (§5.1), scaled ×1.4; on a Quest it's anchored to a detected horizontal plane (the user's table).

### 7.1 The Chart Table (root)
- Paper chart disc **Ø 70 cm** resting on the walnut table in VR (the real table on a Quest), 2 mm above the surface; deckled edge; 4 brass corner weights.
- Around the edge: a **caption strip** (paper ribbon, 60 cm long, 4 cm tall) facing the Organizer — shows the latest full line in Source Serif.
- **Compass rose** printed at the chart's west edge beside the Captain = Watch timer (see 7.5). (As built: from the Organizer's seat the north edge sits behind the globe, so the Captain moved west.)

### 7.2 The Globe
- Paper globe **Ø 32 cm** on a brass meridian ring and wooden stand, centered.
- Oceans: `--sea-wash` watercolor texture; land: `--paper`; coastlines + graticules: `--ink`.
- Candidate cities: ink **pins** with little paper flags (city name in Caslon).
- Home port: **one** brass rivet for the whole crew (the centroid of the three home airports, `HOME_PORT`); each member's airport is never shown, since it pins their flight price (S2-002). During negotiation, **pencil arcs** (graphite) run from the home-port rivet to the city being discussed; agreed arcs turn to **ink**.
- Interaction: auto‑rotates to the city under discussion (ease 600 ms). As built there is no hand rotation (doc 03 §4).

### 7.3 Crew pieces (Advocates)
- Carved walnut pieces, **9 cm tall**, like chess pawns crossed with ship figureheads; one per member.
- Collar ring painted in the member's **band color**; a small paper **name flag** on a brass pin.
- Absent member's piece: same, with a **folded letter** tucked under the flag (they're represented by letter).
- The Captain: a taller brass‑and‑wood piece (12 cm) with a spyglass, positioned at the compass rose on the west edge. Crew seats (as built): Organizer at 180° (south, facing the globe), others at 205°/335°/355° so no piece hides behind the globe.
- **Speaking state:** the piece **tips forward 12°** and slides 2 cm toward the globe; an **ink ribbon** unrolls from its base across the chart (handwritten ≤ 8 words, e.g. "Lisbon, for the beach").
- **Conceding:** piece bows (tips 20°) and slides back; its pencil arc erases.
- **Objecting:** piece turns 15° away; a red ink margin mark appears next to the contested item.
- **Idle:** perfectly still (no breathing/bobbing).

### 7.4 Ink ribbons (replaces chat bubbles)
- A paper ribbon 2 cm tall, length fits text (max 30 cm), unrolls in 400 ms from the piece base in a gentle curve.
- Text in Homemade Apple (≤ 8 words); full line in caption strip + phone log.
- Old ribbons roll back up after the next speaker starts (keeps chart clean).

### 7.5 Compass‑rose Watch timer
- 3 brass pointers = 3 Watches; the active Watch pointer sweeps as each advocate speaks.
- When the Captain calls the decision: pointer snaps to N, **ship's bell** rings.

### 7.6 Dry Run cloches
- Two glass cloches **Ø 26 cm**, on brass bases, slide out from under the chart to left/right of the globe (as built: centers at x = ±0.30 m, z = 0.15 m from the chart center, toward the Organizer).
- Inside each: a disc of the city (Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles clipped to radius; fallback: low‑poly procedural blocks in `--paper` with ink roads).
- **Brass carriage clock** between cloches shows in‑trip time (Plex Mono), ticks audibly each "hour".
- **Tokens:** as built, unlabeled ink beads walk the **group-level** route (one neutral bead per pick) at the public plan's indicative times — no band colours or names, because the public plan never says who goes where or when anyone lands (SEC-001, S2-002; doc 03 §4). Each member's own day is on their phone.
- **Paper tag** under each cloche: city · hotel · group-total **range** (e.g. "$2,650–3,100"), never an exact total.
- **Margin notes:** red ink handwriting next to issues: "30 min uphill", "overnight flight".
- **Edge arrows:** stops outside the cloche radius (e.g. Cascais, Belém, Frida Kahlo museum) are drawn as an ink arrow on the cloche rim labelled with travel time ("to Cascais · 55 min"); beads walk to the rim and wait there.
- Selecting a Chart: select its cloche (a 1.6 s gaze in VR, a pinch on a Quest, or the organizer's phone) and it lifts; the other slides back under the chart (as built there is no grab‑and‑lift, doc 03 §4).

### 7.7 The Seal ceremony
- The chosen Chart appears as a **rolled paper chart** that unrolls on the table, listing each member's share line (Plex Mono) — **amounts only shown to their owner on their phone; on the shared chart shares show as "— sealed —"** until booked.
- Each member sets their seal (phone). On the table, a **wax seal drops and presses** onto that member's line (400 ms press, 60 ms squash) — at the tap, before any card is checked; a lifted seal stays pressed too.
- A moment (about 2.5 s) after the last seal is set, the outcome lands for everyone at once (S2-001). All authorized → chart rolls up, tied with twine, bell rings twice → "Logged in the ship's book".
- Any decline (or the deadline / a call-off) → every seal that was set **cracks together**, then all lift off (voided), ribbon: "No one was charged." As built, no single seal is ever singled out: whose seal failed is private to its owner (SEC-002).

### 7.8 Spectator ("Gallery") view
- Same scene, laptop browser, camera slowly orbits at 30°/min; picture‑in‑picture of the active speaker's name + caption at bottom in a paper strip.

## 8. Phone UI components

Layout: single column, 20px side gutters, ruled paper background (faint `--paper-deep` lines every 26px, a red margin line at 36px on the left like a logbook).

## 9. Motion

| Motion | Duration | Easing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Piece tip (speak) | 250 ms | `cubic-bezier(.3,.7,.4,1)` | + 2 cm slide |
| Ink ribbon unroll | 400 ms | ease‑out | Text writes on over 300 ms (clip reveal left→right) |
| Pencil → ink line | 500 ms | linear draw | Stroke‑dashoffset style reveal |
| Globe auto‑rotate | 600 ms | ease‑in‑out | Never spins continuously |
| Cloche slide‑out | 700 ms | ease‑out | With glass clink sound |
| Wax seal press | 400 ms + 60 ms squash | ease‑in then spring (1 overshoot) | + thud sound |
| Card enter (phone) | 180 ms | ease‑out | Slide 8px up; no fade‑in from 0 opacity > 80% |
| Seal crack | 300 ms | step | Two halves separate 3 mm |

Reduced motion: respect `prefers-reduced-motion` on phones → no slides, instant state changes; in XR, a settings toggle halves distances.

## 11. Voice & copy

Tone: a friendly quartermaster — concise, warm, a little nautical, never cute on every line. **Max one nautical word per sentence.**

| Situation | Copy |
|---|---|
| Brief header | "Your sealed terms" |
| Brief footer | "Only your mate sees this. Not your friends. Not the Captain." |
| After sealing brief | "Sealed. Your mate knows what you can do." |
| Waiting | "Waiting on Dev's terms…" (hourglass icon) |
| Product name | **All Ayes** — set in Libre Caslon Display, small caps, with a wax‑seal "A" as the mark. Never "AllAyes" or "All‑Ayes". |
| Tagline | "Everyone's in, or nobody pays." |
| Descriptor (UI) | "Send your mate to the table." (pitch/Devpost may say "agent"; the UI never does) |
| Negotiation start | Captain: "Mar 12 to 16 works for everyone. Three ports on the chart. Let's hear it." |
| Advocate can't afford (privacy‑safe) | "That's past what my friend can do." (never a number) |
| Captain decision | "Two charts, then. Let's run them dry." |
| Fit (private) | "Fits your terms ✓" / "Over your terms — your mate will push back." |
| Seal button | "Set your seal" |
| Booked | "Logged. Nobody fronted a cent." |
| Voided | "One share didn't clear, so nobody was charged. Try again when ready." (never say whose) |
| Error (network) | "Lost the signal. Holding your place." |

Banned copy: "AI", "magic", "smart", "powered by", "Let me help you with that!", exclamation marks in system copy (max one per flow, on Booked).

## 12. Accessibility

- Every spoken line captioned (caption strip in XR, log on phone, subtitle strip in Spectator).
- Everything doable **without a headset** (phone + Spectator).
- Tap targets ≥ 48 × 48 px; dial also accepts ± buttons and typed input.
- Color is never the only signal: red issues also have a margin note text; fit uses ✓/✗ glyph + words.
- XR: seated‑friendly (all within 60 cm reach), no locomotion, no fast camera motion; comfort toggle for reduced motion.
- Contrast per §3.4.
